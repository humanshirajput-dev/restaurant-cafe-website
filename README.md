# 🍽️ Taste Router
### Explore Restaurants & Discover Popular Cafés

Taste Router is a multi-page restaurant and café discovery website designed to help users explore popular dining places, learn about their facilities, and find their exact locations.

## 🌟 Website Pages

### 🏠 Home
- Attractive homepage with restaurant-related images.
- Introduction to the website.
- Informative content for visitors.

### 🍴 Restaurants
- Collection of different restaurants.
- Restaurant images and descriptions.
- Opening and closing timings.
- Exact locations through Google Maps links.

### ☕ Cafés
- Explore popular cafés.
- Café images and descriptions.
- Opening timings.
- Google Maps location links.
- Approximate cost for one or two people.

### ℹ️ About
- Information about the website.
- A 36-second video tutorial explaining how to use Taste Router.
- Guidance on opening restaurant and café locations directly in Google Maps.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- HTML5

## 🎯 Key Highlights
- Multi-page website structure.
- Restaurant and café discovery.
- Google Maps integration through direct links.
- Images and detailed location information.
- Website usage tutorial video.

## 🎥 How It Works
Users can explore restaurants and cafés, read their descriptions, check timings and estimated costs, and open their locations directly in Google Maps.
